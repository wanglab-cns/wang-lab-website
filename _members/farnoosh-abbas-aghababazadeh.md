Farnoosh earned her Ph.D. in Mathematics with a specialization in Biostatistics from the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Ottawa, Canada. Her doctoral research focused on advancing statistical methods for multiple hypothesis testing using the local false discovery rate, with applications in high-throughput biological data and genome-wide association studies. She also holds an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Mathematical Statistics and Statistics, respectively, from Shiraz University, Iran. Following her Ph.D., Farnoosh joined the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at the Moffitt Cancer Center in Florida as a postdoctoral research fellow. During this time, she gained substantial expertise in statistical genomics and collaborative research involving multi-omics data integration. Currently, Farnoosh’s research centers on developing innovative statistical and computational methodologies to tackle critical challenges in cancer immuno-oncology, cancer epidemiology, and pharmacogenomics. Her work aims to bridge multi-omics data with clinical insights, advancing the fields of precision medicine and translational cancer research.
