Andrew earned his Ph.D. from the University of Toronto, where his doctoral research focused on integrating machine learning and evolutionary principles to model gene regulation and cis-regulatory mechanisms. Prior to his graduate studies, he completed a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a specialization in Bioinformatics at the University of Waterloo. Following his undergraduate degree, Andrew worked as a software developer at the Ontario Institute for Cancer Research (OICR), where he gained extensive expertise in scientific workflow development, pipeline orchestration, and computational reproducibility. Currently, Andrew is a postdoctoral research fellow leveraging multimodal single-cell genomics to investigate glioblastoma and other gliomas. His research aims to uncover cellular heterogeneity and decipher disease mechanisms, bridging deep learning architectures with high-throughput multi-omics to advance cancer biology and precision oncology.
